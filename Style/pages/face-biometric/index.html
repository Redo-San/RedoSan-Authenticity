Face Biometric Rights

🔒 All face processing is 100% local. Data never leaves your device.

💡 How to use Face Biometric ▾
Register and verify face biometrics for visual-rights protection — 100% in your browser, nothing is uploaded. 1) Read the Biometric Consent Notice and tick the box to enable capture. 2) On the Register tab, upload a photo or use your camera; the tool extracts a 512-d face descriptor and a privacy-preserving BioHash. 3) Optional but recommended: register a passkey (WebAuthn). Your stored templates are then encrypted with a key derived from that passkey, so they stay unreadable without it. 4) On the Verify tab, compare a new photo against your registry to find a match. 5) On the Manage tab you can export an encrypted backup, import it on another device, or delete entries. ⚠️ All processing is on-device. Withdraw consent at any time to delete every stored biometric record (GDPR Art. 7(3) + 17).

Biometric Consent Notice

This tool processes facial geometry — a biometric identifier — entirely in your browser. Please read the details below before continuing.

  • What: a numeric face descriptor (512-d embedding) derived from your photo, plus a Privacy ID (BioHash) generated from it.
  • Purpose: verifying and protecting your visual rights — identity verification, anti-spoofing and forgery detection.
  • Storage: in this browser, on this device only. There is no server or cloud — nothing is uploaded, shared or used for training.
  • Retention: records stay in this browser's local storage on this device until you delete them or withdraw consent; a local safety net also removes records left unused for 3 years.
  • Your rights: you may withdraw consent at any time — this deletes all stored biometric data from this device.
You can browse the site without consenting — biometric features stay disabled.
✅ Consent recorded. You can withdraw it anytime — withdrawing deletes your stored biometric data.

🎥 Liveness blocks photo/screen replay (ISO/IEC 30107-3 style). Registration via camera requires a passing check.

ArcFace loads a ~13 MB ONNX model from jsDelivr on first use (WebGPU/WASM). Human runs fully offline.
